NPEC Root Phenotyping

for NPEC · Individual & group projects · Nov 2025 – Jun 2026

Problem

Plant researchers need to inoculate root tips precisely, follow them over time, and run their computer vision models reliably in different environments.

Approach

A CV-to-control pipeline: U-Net segmentation and skeletonization locate root tips, and a controller steers a simulated robot’s pipette. I compared PID against PPO reinforcement learning and chose PID. In separate work I benchmarked 5 tracking methods against a baseline with repeated-measures ANOVA, and built a CI/CD pipeline that publishes one Docker image running locally, on-premise and on Azure, with Airflow retraining and MLflow-tracked model promotion.

Result

96% end-to-end success with sub-millimeter precision, ranked top 10% in the associated Kaggle competition. The tracking benchmark found no significant improvement over the baseline, a rigorously validated null result.

Ukrainian Video Emotion Classification

for CIA · Group project · Sep – Nov 2025

Problem

Tag emotions in Ukrainian-language video, a low-resource language with little labelled training data.

Approach

An end-to-end pipeline: Whisper transcription, optional translation, then classification with a fine-tuned Ukrainian RoBERTa transformer. We generated synthetic training data with a local LLM and documented the model with model cards, privacy and sustainability reporting.

Result

78% accuracy on the 7-class emotion task.

  • Whisper
  • RoBERTa
  • HuggingFace Transformers
  • Synthetic data
  • Model cards

FingerSpell: Dutch Sign Language Recognition

Group project · Jan – Feb 2026

Problem

Help learners practise the NGT (Dutch Sign Language) finger alphabet, including letters that involve movement.

Approach

A real-time webcam tool with hand tracking, built in a 2-week sprint with two teammates. The first model handled static hand shapes well; we improved it with engineered motion features and classical ML model selection.

Result

Near-100% accuracy across the full alphabet.

  • OpenCV
  • Feature engineering

Dynamic Pricing & Demand Forecasting

for Move Intermodal · Group project · Sep 2026 – ongoing

Problem

Move Intermodal, a European intermodal logistics provider, wants pricing that responds to the market and fills more rail capacity.

Approach

With a cross-disciplinary team, building a Dynamic Pricing Strategy Framework that combines market-signal analysis, customer segmentation and a demand forecasting model.

Result

Ongoing. The project targets a 10–15% revenue increase and 5–10% higher rail occupancy; these are goals, not achieved results yet.
